# Curated illustration sources

Historical sourcing notes from August 2026. The live site now uses 38 original
engraved SVGs in `illustrations/`; see [their provenance](./illustrations/PROVENANCE.md).
The stock assets below are retained as reference material and are not rendered by the app.

This is a source map for every item in the study. It deliberately avoids copying
brand packaging: the branded cereal entries use neutral cereal illustrations.

## Primary sources

- [Food Icon Pack — Classic](https://foodiconpack.com/themes/classic): SVGs from
  the Common Ingredient and Common Dishes packs are CC BY 4.0. Credit Food Icon
  Pack, link to the licence, and note any treatment or adaptation.
- [Tasty Icons Free](https://handdrawngoods.com/tasty-icons-free-food-icons) by
  Hand-Drawn Goods: the local `tasty-icons-free` vectors already used in the site
  are covered by the creator's Creative Commons Attribution licence.
- [FoodShift](https://www.instantshift.com/2014/12/24/foodshift-free-food-drink-icon-set/):
  free SVG pack designed by Freepik for InstantShift. Keep the source-page credit;
  do not redistribute the full pack.
- [FreeSVG](https://freesvg.org/): the selected assets are marked CC0/Public
  Domain by the site. The links below lead to each asset's landing page, where the
  SVG download is available.

## Art-direction decision

The external CC BY and CC0 sets above give complete, legal coverage, but their
line art is materially simpler than the engraved reference supplied for this
project. The site therefore continues to use its more detailed local sketch
drawings, plus the six matching Tasty vectors already integrated. Replace a
local drawing only after visual review; a licence-safe source alone is not enough
to keep the illustration system coherent.
