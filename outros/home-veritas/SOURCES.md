## Official resource library

- [First-home support](https://www.govt.nz/browse/housing-and-property/buying-or-selling-a-home/buying-your-first-home/) — Check KiwiSaver withdrawal and First Home Loan routes. Confirm eligibility before counting on the money.
- [Can you legally buy?](https://www.linz.govt.nz/guidance/overseas-investment/buying-residential-property-live) — LINZ’s guidance on citizenship, residency and when overseas investment consent may be needed.
- [Choosing a mortgage adviser](https://www.fma.govt.nz/consumer/getting-advice/mortgage-advice/) — Questions about lender coverage, commissions, fees, conflicts and getting advice.
- [Titles, plans & interests](https://lrs.linz.govt.nz/) — Find title records and registered documents. Ask your lawyer to interpret easements, covenants and restrictions.
- [What to ask the council](https://www.building.govt.nz/getting-started/your-rights-and-obligations/homeowner-rights-and-obligations/buying-a-house/what-can-the-council-tell-you-about-the-house) — LIM, property file, plans, consents and completion records. A LIM and a building inspection answer different questions.
- [Natural hazards](https://www.settled.govt.nz/buying-a-home/researching-the-property/understanding-the-impact-of-natural-hazards/) — Look into flooding, coastal, earthquake and other hazards. Confirm cover for the specific address before committing.
- [Previous damage & claims](https://www.naturalhazards.govt.nz/insurance-and-claims/claims/buying-or-selling-a-home-with-previous-claims/) — Check repairs and the transfer of NHC/EQC claims. A paid claim is not proof that the damage was repaired.
- [Do I need consent?](https://www.canibuildit.govt.nz/) — Start with MBIE’s tool, then confirm your exact project with the council. Exempt work still has requirements.
- [Building & planning approvals](https://www.building.govt.nz/projects-and-consents/planning-a-successful-build/scope-and-design/check-if-you-need-consents) — Check building consent, resource consent and other permissions. One approval does not replace the others.
- [Completion & missing sign-off](https://www.building.govt.nz/projects-and-consents/sign-off-and-maintenance/completing-your-project/certificate-of-acceptance) — Understand a certificate of acceptance and its limits when work was done without the required building consent.
- [Buying a unit title](https://www.unittitles.govt.nz/buying-or-selling-a-unit-title/buying-or-selling-a-unit-title/buying-a-unit-title/) — Disclosure statements, body corporate information, maintenance funding and shared responsibilities.
- [Building Act 2004](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2004/72/en/latest/) — The legislation behind building work, consents and compliance. Use the current version with professional advice.
- [Unit Titles Act 2010](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2010/0022/latest/DLM1160440.html) — The legislation on unit ownership, body corporates, financial management and disclosure obligations.
- [Understand the contract](https://www.settled.govt.nz/buying-a-home/making-an-offer/understanding-the-sale-and-purchase-agreement-when-buying/) — Read about the sale and purchase agreement, conditions and the commitment you are making.
- [Asbestos: check before disturbing](https://www.healthnz.govt.nz/health-topics/keeping-healthy/healthy-homes-environments/hazardous-substances/asbestos/dealing-with-asbestos) — Health New Zealand guidance on suspected asbestos and professional assessment or removal.
- [Who represents you?](https://www.settled.govt.nz/buying-a-home/finding-a-property/buying-with-an-agent-or-privately/) — Selling agents, buyer’s agents, private owners and the checks to make before signing.
- [Check an agent’s licence](https://publicregister.rea.govt.nz/) — REA’s public register of licensed real estate professionals.
- [Agent conduct and disclosures](https://www.settled.govt.nz/assets/Uploads/Resources/Guides/Code-of-conduct.pdf) — Professional conduct, client care and disclosure requirements, including known and likely hidden defects.
- [Tender rules and deposits](https://www.settled.govt.nz/buying-a-home/making-an-offer/buying-by-tender/) — Confidential offers, conditions, deposit arrangements and possible early sales.
- [Deadline sales and early offers](https://www.settled.govt.nz/buying-a-home/making-an-offer/buying-by-deadline/) — Understand closing dates and what “unless sold prior” can mean.
- [Competing written offers](https://www.settled.govt.nz/buying-a-home/making-an-offer/understanding-a-multi-offer-process/) — How a multi-offer process works and why another chance is not guaranteed.
