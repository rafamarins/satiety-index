## Source review

New content checked 26 October 2026 against primary guidance. An independent source review found no must-fix factual errors; three useful refinements were incorporated: buyer’s agent hired/paid by the buyer under a written agreement, auction rules possibly continuing after a pass-in, and a mortgagee sale potentially being removed if the owner repays before settlement.

- [Settled — agents and private owners](https://www.settled.govt.nz/buying-a-home/finding-a-property/buying-with-an-agent-or-privately/): representation, fairness, disclosures, written agreements, deposit trust handling and limits of the REA agent-complaints route for private unlicensed sellers.
- [REA — buyer’s agent guidance](https://www.rea.govt.nz/real-estate-professionals/industry-guidance-and-information/buyers-agent-information/): written buyer agency and prohibition on the same agent/agency representing both parties in the same transaction. Helping introduce a buyer to a listing is not itself buyer representation.
- [Conduct and client care rules](https://www.settled.govt.nz/assets/Uploads/Resources/Guides/Code-of-conduct.pdf): rules on fairness, pressure, price information, advice and known/likely hidden defects. This is not a guarantee that every defect is known.
- [Negotiation and price guides](https://www.settled.govt.nz/buying-a-home/making-an-offer/buying-by-negotiation/), [deadline sales](https://www.settled.govt.nz/buying-a-home/making-an-offer/buying-by-deadline/), [tenders](https://www.settled.govt.nz/buying-a-home/making-an-offer/buying-by-tender/), [auction](https://www.settled.govt.nz/buying-a-home/making-an-offer/buying-by-auction/) and [multi-offer processes](https://www.settled.govt.nz/buying-a-home/making-an-offer/understanding-a-multi-offer-process/): process differences and qualifications. No promise that a particular seller accepts conditions, waits until a date or accepts the highest offer.
- [Property options](https://www.settled.govt.nz/buying-a-home/finding-a-property/considering-your-property-options/): mortgagee risks, access, possession, warranties, inclusions, insurance and possible repayment before settlement.
- [Estates and trusts](https://www.settled.govt.nz/selling-a-home/thinking-of-selling/planning-to-sell/): need for legal advice on authority and circumstances. The guide does not invent a universal probate/signing rule.
- [REA public register](https://publicregister.rea.govt.nz/): useful destination linked by Settled. The research tool could not directly retrieve this endpoint; no individual licence or service-availability verification is claimed.

Advice remains general and property-specific legal review is repeatedly signposted. These are the main sale processes and useful special cases; no claim of exhaustive coverage of every contract variant.
