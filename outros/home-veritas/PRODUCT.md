# Home Veritas

## Purpose

Make the NZ first-home journey easy to understand: a big visual, one clear idea, a useful next action. Keep the complete supporting material available without placing it all on the first screen.

## Approved direction

Option 1 from the October 2026 concept exploration: lemon yellow, cobalt blue, oversized condensed black headings, realistic photographic cut-paper collage and white reading surfaces. The user approved this direction after seeing neighbourhood, broker and inspection sketches.

## Experience

Six broad interest areas lead to seventeen topics. The home screen presents large visual blocks; an area opens related topic blocks; a topic opens three expandable questions. Answers stay in place rather than requiring sequential card navigation. Always allow free navigation, searching and direct access to deeper guidance and official sources. A topic should explain why it matters and make the next step clear; exploration is never an eligibility quiz or readiness score.

## Principles

- Real readable text and working controls, never a screenshot presented as an application.
- The visual introduces the subject; selected information has clear reading space.
- Stable navigation; no camera flights or zoom that hides the scene or controls.
- Short first layer, then gotchas, examples, FAQs, exceptions and official evidence. Use photographic collage comparisons or sequences where they explain a distinction; pair each scene with a short caption. Retain complete sourced wording in short blocks. Keep the experience focused on learning and source access; no saved answers, personal notes, completion controls or exports.
- Collages are explanatory illustrations, not property evidence or technical surveys.
- Mobile users can reach the same content and source library. Respect zoom, keyboard use and reduced motion.
- Keep the first load light. No continuous animation or 3D runtime is needed for this direction.

## Language

English, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish and Simplified Chinese cover the whole guide, including deeper explanations and FAQs. Preserve NZ document names where useful and all source URLs. External official websites may be English-only.

## Scope

Deciding, costs, deposit/KiwiSaver, bank/broker/legal team, mortgage, real estate agents and representation, neighbourhood, home/title types, viewings, inspections, council/property records, consents, hazards/insurance, sale methods, contracts, settlement, maintenance and renovation. Professional advice and property-specific checks remain essential.
